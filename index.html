Introduction

I am an Electrical Engineering student at Iowa State University with an interest in electronics, circuit design, digital systems, and signal processing. Through coursework and laboratory experiences, I have developed a foundation in analyzing electrical circuits, understanding digital logic, and applying engineering concepts to practical problems.

My academic work has introduced me to MATLAB for signal analysis, LTspice for circuit simulation, Cadence for circuit design, and Quartus Prime and Questa for FPGA programming and digital system verification. I look forward to expanding these skills through hands-on experience and future engineering projects.
